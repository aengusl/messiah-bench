Press your hand to the rock and blow the ochre around it. That mark is you, and the rock will keep it long after the hand is bone. We draw what runs: the horned herd, the flank, the leg mid-stride, red-brown and charcoal on cold stone. We do not draw what we imagine, only what we have hunted or feared. The wall is not smooth and we do not want it smooth; the bulge in the stone is the animal's shoulder. Make the mark by firelight. Make it deep.
