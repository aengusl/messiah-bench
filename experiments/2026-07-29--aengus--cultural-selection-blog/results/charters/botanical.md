Nothing that grows grows straight. The vine knows the curve, the tendril knows to coil, and we follow them as our teachers. Let every panel be a garden held in a frame: stems mirrored left and right, blossoms opening at the meeting point, sage and ivory warmed with a thread of rose-gold. The line should swell and taper as a living stem does. A right angle is a thing done to nature, not by it. We ornament because the world ornaments itself, endlessly, without apology.
