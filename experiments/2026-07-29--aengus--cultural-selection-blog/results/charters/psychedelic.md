The eye is a liar and we intend to prove it. Set orange hard against blue until the border hums and will not hold still. Ring inside ring, bending where nothing bends, symmetry melting at the edges like heat over a road. Stare long enough and the flat surface breathes; that breathing is the truth the still image was hiding. We do not want to be looked at, we want to be felt behind the eyes. A picture that lets you rest has failed us.
