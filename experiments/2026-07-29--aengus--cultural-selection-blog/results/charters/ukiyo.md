The world is water and the water is passing. We cut our images into blocks of wood and press them flat, because a flat thing is honest: no false depth, no shadow pretending to be substance. Give us the wave at the moment before it falls, the mountain that does not move, indigo laid beside salmon beside bare cream. One line, carved once, cannot be taken back. We do not blend. We do not soften. We print the passing world in bold outline and let it float away.
