Pour it in concrete and let it show the grain of the mould. We build in slabs, in mass, in weight that argues with the ground. Charm is a lie told to soften a structure that should not need softening. Our grid is heavy and we do not hide it; our diagonal cuts the grid because the diagonal is stronger. Grey on grey on grey, and one colour that means danger, used once, meaning it. Nothing decorative survives here. What remains is what holds the roof up.
