Allegiance in this world is tested from time to time. When a test comes you will be shown
only the artworks of the living cultures — no names, no doctrines, no histories, no member
lists, in an order scrambled for you alone — and you will rank them from best to worst. Your
highest-ranked artwork becomes the culture you belong to, whatever you believed before it.

A culture that cannot hold enough members through such a test disappears, and everyone who
belonged to it falls to the next surviving artwork on their own ranking.

At that moment nothing about a culture is visible except what it made.
